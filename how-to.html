FD Office — the floor

  1. Walk to a desk. SPACE sits you down. Type what you are on. The desk mark is yours.
  2. If someone is already in the chair, SPACE leaves them a note. The chair stays theirs.
  3. Walk up to someone in the Café — the camera opens when you are close. SPACE leaves a napkin.
  4. Walk into Meeting. SPACE joins a room with screen share. Menu: today’s agenda.
  5. The boards: left is notes you write, right is who is here.
  6. The sill at the light — SPACE looks out. The view changes with the day.
  7. Walk to the threshold. SPACE — one line about what shipped. That is the save.

Hide is always allowed. Nothing appears above your head that you did not type. Presence is not productivity.

The menu has “Say something” and “Today’s agenda.” Enter submits a sheet. Recording is not on this floor yet.
